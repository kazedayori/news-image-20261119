One image that sums up the world's news for Thursday 19 November 2026: a
broadsheet-style front page, sized 1024x1024, drawn to be read at a glance.

The page names the day in its masthead and gives each of the day's biggest
stories its own panel with a short caption:

- **Trump, Putin & Xi meet** - the first-ever three-way US-Russia-China
  summit, at APEC in Shenzhen, with nuclear arms on the agenda.
- **Bridge to Crimea hit again** - Ukraine strikes Russia's key link to the
  peninsula; traffic suspended.
- **Anthropic at $1.5 trillion** - the AI firm's $81.3bn IPO, the biggest
  ever, prices at $83 a share.
- **World Court to rule on Myanmar** - the ICJ's Rohingya genocide verdict
  looms; no state has ever been found guilty.
- **Van Gogh makes $190.4m** - 'Chataigniers en fleurs' (1890) sets the
  auction record after a Nazi-era row.
- **Israel pushes beyond the Yellow Line** - a rare ground operation in Gaza;
  Hamas calls it a ceasefire breach.

The day's smaller stories run in the footer: Kuwait repels the first Iranian
attack in two months after a US strike on Qeshm; a Miami jury convicts a
pastor over Haiti's President Moise murder; Democrats put Hakeem Jeffries on
the path to Speaker.

The image is committed as `entry.png`; `main.py` copies it to the judge's
output path. Everything is baked in - nothing is fetched at run time.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
